# Blogs page image sources

The image files under `public/images/blog/` are locally optimized WebP copies used by the Blogs page. The linked source pages identify the photographers and applicable free-use licenses.

- **Bagan sunrise** (`bagan-sunrise.webp`): Daniel Lienert, [Pexels photo](https://www.pexels.com/photo/hot-air-balloons-flying-above-buddhist-temple-at-sunrise-in-myanmar-18152639/). Pexels identifies the photo as free to use; attribution is appreciated but not required.
- **Shwedagon evening** (`shwedagon-evening.webp`): farfar, [Unsplash photo](https://unsplash.com/photos/schwedagon-pagoda-myanmar-PU801Af3FGg), free under the Unsplash License.
- **Inle stilt houses** (`inle-stilt-houses.webp`): Steph Smith, [Unsplash photo](https://unsplash.com/photos/houses-above-sea-water-during-daytime-dM5d51e3D38), free under the Unsplash License.
- **U Bein Bridge** (`ubein-evening.webp`): matthulland, [Pixabay photo](https://pixabay.com/photos/u-bein-bridge-mandalay-myanmar-1861037/), free under the Pixabay Content License.
